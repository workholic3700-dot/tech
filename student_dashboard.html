How matching works

RumCHUO first checks the essential requirements for shared accommodation. Students are only matched when they meet all the mandatory requirements below.

Mandatory requirements
  • You and the potential roommate must be from the same university.
  • You and the potential roommate must have the same gender.
  • Both students must have selected shared accommodation.

After these requirements are met, RumCHUO uses the following details to improve the match score:

  • Preferred accommodation location.
  • Course or programme of study.
  • Year of study.

Year of study is a scoring factor by default. You can optionally restrict results to students in your exact year.
